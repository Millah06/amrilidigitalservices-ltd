Refund Policy

Effective Date: 17th February 2026  |  Last Updated: 1st March 2026

1. Overview

Amrili Digital Services Limited ("the Company") is committed to delivering reliable, high-quality digital services and marketplace experiences. This Refund Policy sets out the circumstances under which refunds are issued across our services, and the process for submitting a refund request.

2. Software Development and Consulting Services

For custom software development, IT consulting, and professional services engaged under a written service agreement:

  • Milestone-based projects: Payments made for completed and approved milestones are non-refundable.
  • Retainer or advance payments: Where work has not yet commenced, you may request a refund within 5 business days of payment. Refunds will be assessed based on any preparatory work already undertaken.
  • Service failure: Where we materially fail to deliver agreed deliverables and cannot remedy the failure within a reasonable cure period, a pro-rated refund may be issued for undelivered work.

3. Marketplace Transactions

For purchases made through our digital marketplace platform:

  • Non-delivery: If an order is confirmed and payment is collected but the seller fails to deliver the product or service, you are entitled to a full refund. Report non-delivery within 48 hours of the expected delivery time.
  • Significantly not as described: If a product or service is materially different from how it was listed on our platform, you may be eligible for a refund. Report within 24 hours of receipt with photographic or documented evidence.
  • Completed and accepted orders: Orders confirmed as delivered and accepted by the buyer are final and non-refundable.
  • Food and perishable goods: Due to the perishable nature of food and grocery items, refunds are only available for non-delivery or clear quality failure reported immediately upon receipt.

4. Failed Transactions

If a payment is debited from your account but the corresponding service, order, or transaction was not completed or recorded on our platform:

  • Report the failed transaction within 24 hours by providing your transaction reference, bank statement extract showing the debit, and a description of the service attempted.
  • We will investigate within 3 business days and, where confirmed, process a refund to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

5. Duplicate Charges

If you are charged more than once for the same transaction, report it immediately with documentation of both charges. Duplicate charges will be refunded in full within 5 business days of confirmation.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • You provided incorrect information (e.g., wrong delivery address, wrong order details) and the service was delivered as specified.
  • You changed your mind after a service was delivered or an order was confirmed as received.
  • The refund request is submitted outside the applicable reporting window specified in this policy.
  • The issue is due to factors outside our control, including third-party payment gateway delays, network failures, or your own device/account issues.
  • The account has been suspended or terminated for violation of our Terms and Conditions.

To submit a refund request, contact us through any of the following channels:

  • Email: info@amrilidigitalservices.com — with subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Transaction Reference]"

Please include the following in your request:

  • Full name and registered email address.
  • Transaction reference or order ID.
  • Date and amount of the transaction.
  • Clear description of the issue and reason for the refund request.
  • Supporting evidence (screenshots, photos, transaction confirmation) where applicable.

8. Refund Processing Timeline

  • We will acknowledge your request within 1 business day.
  • Investigation and decision within 3–5 business days.
  • Approved refunds processed to original payment method within 5–10 business days.
  • Refund timeline for bank transfers may vary based on the receiving bank.

9. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our refund decision, you may escalate the matter by emailing info@amrilidigitalservices.com with "Formal Dispute" in the subject line. Unresolved disputes are subject to the dispute resolution process outlined in our Terms and Conditions.
