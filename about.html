Who We Are

Technology Built for Nigeria, Designed for the World

Amrili Digital Services Limited is a privately owned Nigerian technology company incorporated in February 2026 under the Corporate Affairs Commission (RC: 9339018). We are headquartered in Azare, Bauchi State, and operate as a remote-first software development and digital platform company.

Our focus is building software products and digital platforms that solve real problems for businesses and individuals across Nigeria. We specialize in marketplace platforms, mobile applications, and custom software development — integrating licensed payment processors and third-party service APIs to deliver complete, production-ready digital solutions.

We are a lean, mission-driven team committed to building things properly — with clean architecture, regulatory compliance, and genuine usefulness as our baseline standards.
